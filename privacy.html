Typica · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026.

Typica is a private assistant operated for a single household. Only Google accounts belonging to that household are authorised to use it. It is not offered to the public.

Data Typica accesses

With your explicit consent through Google's sign-in screen, Typica may read and act on data in the Google services you approve, such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Tasks. It only requests the permissions shown on that screen.

How the data is used

  • Solely to carry out tasks you ask for, such as summarising email, preparing your agenda, or keeping your own records up to date.
  • Data is processed on a private server controlled by the household and by the AI model provider used to answer your requests.
  • Data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.
  • Google user data is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on that private server with owner-only file permissions. Backups of the server are encrypted.

Your choices

You can revoke Typica's access at any time from your Google Account under Third-party connections. After revocation, Typica can no longer reach your Google data. You may also ask the operator to delete any records kept on your behalf.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Typica's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on Typica's Google sign-in screen.
