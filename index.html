Est. 1696 · Batavia

Typica

The heirloom coffee that crossed an ocean, outlived a flood, and became the root of most of the world's arabica.

  1. 1696The first arabica seedlings reach Batavia from Malabar. A flood takes them before they can grow.
  2. 1699A second shipment arrives and survives. These Typica plants become the coffee the world will simply call Java.
  3. 1706A single plant from Java is sent to the botanical garden in Amsterdam.
  4. 1714One of its descendants is given to Louis XIV. From there, Typica travels on to the Caribbean and the Americas.
